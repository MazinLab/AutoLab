## LCARS display theme (2026-07-20)

An LCARS "Starfleet science console" theme, styled after the Mazin Lab main
site and HerriottCellCalc: true-black field with a CRT scanline overlay,
orange chrome and the Okudagram palette, Antonio display caps, Barlow Semi
Condensed body, JetBrains Mono for accessions and numeric readouts, elbow
radii, segmented masthead bars, a colored nav rail, and glowing viewscreen
frames around uploaded figures. Toggle with the LCARS button next to the
actor picker; the choice persists per device and applies before first paint.
The default cream theme is untouched (the skin is fully scoped under
`data-theme="lcars"`), and fonts are self-hosted so the PWA stays
offline-capable. The New Note nav entry was removed (quick notes remain at
/notes/new); LCARS page titles fit one line when there is room and the base
font scale is larger.
