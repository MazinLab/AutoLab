# 2026-07-19 Labels and QR printing

AutoLab now derives deterministic dotted display labels from an entity's
primary `DERIVED_FROM` ancestry chain without storing or using them as
identifiers. Entity pages can print QR or text-only Zebra labels through a
configured network printer, and selected entity-creation flows automatically
print a QR label when a printer is available. ZPL is sent directly over TCP,
normally port 9100, before the append-only `label_printed` event is recorded;
the physical send and event transaction are therefore not atomic.
