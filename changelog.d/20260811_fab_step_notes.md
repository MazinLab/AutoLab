# Fab steps carry run notes and photos

The Fab Step form now has a markdown notes area and attachment upload
(phone camera photos included). The recipe stays the stable procedure —
etch chemistry, gasses — and whatever changed this run ("36 s at 300 W")
goes in the step's notes next to the pictures. The short-lived per-run
variables field on Fab Recipe is gone in favor of this. Picking a wafer
also prefills Step Index with the next number in that wafer's sequence
(editable).

Every attachment dropzone (Fab Step, Device, Fab Note, equipment, …) now
has a "Take a photo" button that opens the phone camera directly and
attaches the shot in one tap.
